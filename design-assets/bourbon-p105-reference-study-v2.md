# Bourbon Sapphire P105: reference study and render v2

Prepared 1 October 2026. This study accompanies the fresh **with-B** render. The earlier generated concepts were not used as geometry references.

## Research coverage

| Vessel | IMO | Distinct photographic references | Requested minimum | Coverage |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Bourbon Sapphire | 9386691 | 21 | 20 | Met; includes one partly obscured group photo |
| Bourbon Ruby | 9386689 | 19 | 20 | One short; includes 3 deck/partial views and 1 distant view |
| Bourbon Diamond | 9386706 | 14 | 20 | Six short |
| Bourbon Pearl / Parcel do Bandolim | 9386677 | 21 | 20 | Met; 19 photos show the converted vessel |

These are **75 distinct photographic references**, not 75 full exterior views. Copies at other resolutions and photographs of other Bourbon designs are excluded. The requested minimum of 20 for every vessel has not been achieved. Additional public galleries were searched, but unavailable images and inaccessible full-size photographs are not counted. Counts do not include general arrangement drawings, logos, maps, generated images, contact sheets, or boat names found only in text.

The main shape and paint-layout evidence is the Sapphire collection. Sister-vessel photos supplement the accommodation, mast, stern, deck walls, and bow observations. Pearl's conversion photos are used only where the original hull/house is visible, excluding the added helideck and heavy offshore crane.

## Primary identification and specifications

[Ulstein: Bourbon Ruby](https://ulstein.com/references/bourbon-ruby), [Ulstein: Bourbon Diamond](https://ulstein.com/references/bourbon-diamond), and [Ulstein: Bourbon Pearl / Parcel do Bandolim](https://ulstein.com/references/parcel-do-bandolim) identify the P105 design and the four sister vessels. The design's as-built length and beam are 91.1 m and 19 m. They are proportion references, not a measurement of every visible detail in a photo.

Specifications/arrangement documents inspected:

- [Bourbon Ruby P105 specification](https://www.bourbonoffshore.com/sites/default/files/2021-08/psv-bourbon-ruby-p105.pdf). Includes top-deck arrangement and a vessel photo; not a measured side elevation.
- [Bourbon Diamond specification](https://www.bourbonoffshore.com/sites/default/files/2021-08/bourbon-diamond-4850-t.pdf). The illustrated ship photo is labelled Ruby and is not counted as a separate Diamond photograph.
- [Ruby project summary, EPA Guyana](https://epaguyana.org/download/bourbon-ruby-project-summary/). Includes an exterior photo.
- [Diamond project summary, EPA Guyana](https://epaguyana.org/download/mv-bourbon-diamond-project-summary/). Inspected for vessel information.

## Observations used for the fresh render

| Feature | Photo-based direction |
|---|---|
| Bow | Conventional flared P105 bow, forward stem, modest rounded bulb/forefoot. |
| Accommodation | Tall white house, three visible levels below the wheelhouse; broad sloping forward face with the window rows retained. |
| Cargo deck | Long low aft deck bounded by high solid cargo walls; grey inner faces, green outer faces, white guardrails above. |
| Corporate B | One oversized integrated paint graphic crossing both white accommodation and green hull. The white aft vertical stroke, green upper shape, white lower bowl, and vessel-name crossbar follow the starboard photographs. |
| Mast | Tall narrow black open-frame structure with rectangular platforms and antenna/radar fittings. |
| Deck hardware | Yellow rescue/work boat near the aft face of the house; deck fittings and fender layout taken from the photo set. |
| Style | Matte/satin CGI illustration, transparent background, restrained gloss. Starboard forequarter view, bow to the right. |

## Generation input and saved assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using a new prompt and actual vessel photographs. The tool accepts a maximum of five local reference image files. The selected inputs were:

1. `sapphire-vt-986879.jpg`: starboard broadside and B proportions.
2. `sapphire-vt-986800.jpg`: starboard bow/house view.
3. `sapphire-fotoalfi-180509_bourbon-sapphire.jpg`: elevated deck/solid cargo-wall geometry.
4. `ruby-official2000.png`: accommodation levels and integrated B layout.
5. `p105-evidence-contact-sheet.jpg`: the first 20 Sapphire photographs, plus Diamond bow, Ruby stern, and original Pearl comparison views.

The remaining references informed the written brief and comparison. They were not all supplied separately to the generator. The last Ruby Marseille photograph was found after generation and cross-checked against the result.

- `bourbon-sapphire-p105-with-b-v2.png`: original RGBA result, 1774 x 887 pixels.
- `bourbon-sapphire-p105-with-b-v2.webp`: compressed copy at the same pixel dimensions, alpha preserved, quality 92.
- `bourbon-sapphire-p105-with-b-v2-prompt.txt`: exact final prompt used.

This remains a render draft for owner/operator review. It is a generated illustration, not a measured CAD model. Deck count, rail construction, paint scale/location, mast, and bow were visually compared with the sources; small fittings and proportions may still need correction. No no-B variant was generated in this pass.

Source photographs are retained as research originals in a separate private reference archive. They are not added to the public project or packaged in the app. This file records their page URLs, source-image URLs, and uses.
